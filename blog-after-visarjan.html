What happens to the flowers after Ganesh visarjan?

1 October 2026

An idol arrives for immersion wearing days of offerings: marigold garlands, roses, leaves, thread. For a long time all of it went into the lake together.

The flowers are the easiest part of that to save. They are clean organic material, and they only need to be separated before the idol goes to the crane.

What changed in 2026

For the 2026 immersion season in Hyderabad, devotees were asked to hand over floral offerings before immersion. Dedicated bins and collection points were set up at immersion ponds so that garlands and puja materials did not end up in the water.

Pakka Organics ran collection points at immersion sites including IDL Lake in Kukatpally, Saroornagar and Malkajgiri. People walked up with garlands in their hands and passed them across the counter. It took a few seconds each.

Where the flowers went next

Collected flowers were packed in sacks and brought to our drying yard. There they were sorted by hand, spread out in the sun and dried. Dried petals are what we grind to make incense, dhoop and natural colours.

So far we have recycled 19.8 tonnes of flowers this way.

What you can do next year

  • Take the garlands off the idol before it goes for immersion.
  • Keep flowers apart from plastic, foil and cloth.
  • Hand them in at a flower-collection point or kiosk at the immersion site.
