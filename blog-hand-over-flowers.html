How to hand over your puja flowers

3 October 2026

Flowers from a home puja, a temple or a function can be recycled if they reach us in good shape. These four habits make the difference.

Keep flowers on their own

Put flowers in a separate bag or basket. Food, plastic covers, foil and cloth should go elsewhere. The cleaner the bag, the less sorting is needed later.

Keep them dry and aired

A paper bag, cloth bag or open basket works well. Flowers kept in a sealed plastic bag start to rot quickly.

Do not wait too long

Fresh and half-dry flowers are both fine. Flowers that have been left wet for days are not.

Tell us before you bring a large quantity

Temples, function halls and event organisers often have sacks of flowers after a ceremony. Message us on WhatsApp first, and we will tell you where and when to hand them over.

During Ganesh visarjan, look for a flower-collection kiosk at the immersion point and hand over garlands there before the idol goes for immersion.
